Telstra
"Telstra's digital products had grown without a shared foundation. Each team was solving the same problems independently. We needed a single source of truth — a design system that would scale across every surface."

I was brought in to audit Telstra's existing digital product suite and lead the creation of a token-based design system across their consumer and enterprise apps. Working alongside four platform teams and three agencies, I facilitated the consolidation of over 200 unique UI components into a shared, Figma-connected component library — with corresponding React and native implementations.

Situation

Without a shared design language, the digital products suffered from visual inconsistency and duplicated engineering effort. Users experienced jarring transitions between apps, and design-to-dev handoff was slow and error-prone.

Task

Establish a unified design system that would scale across every surface, consolidating over 200 unique UI components into a shared library.

Action

We established a three-tier token architecture (Global → Semantic → Component) and built a cross-platform component library in Figma, React, and React Native. A comprehensive Storybook and documentation site ensured consistent adoption across all teams.

Result

Within six months of launch, the design system was adopted by all major product lines with zero breaking divergence. Design-to-dev handoff time dropped by 60%, and QA consistency scores improved significantly.
